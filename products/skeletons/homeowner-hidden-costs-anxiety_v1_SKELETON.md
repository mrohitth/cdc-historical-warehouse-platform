## PROBLEM DIAGNOSIS

**Core Problem:** New homeowners (especially first-time buyers in their late 30s/early 40s) discover that homeownership costs an average of $21,084 per year beyond their mortgage — and the emotional impact is described as 'nightmare,' 'terrifying,' and financially devastating. They received extensive education about mortgages and down payments but zero preparation for the ongoing maintenance financial reality. The result is buyer regret, anxiety disorders, and families feeling trapped.

**Root Cause Cluster:**
1. **The mortgage education gap** — Buyers are screened and educated on mortgage payments, interest rates, and closing costs. There is no equivalent education on what it costs to MAINTAIN a home after purchase
2. **Systems failure timeline** — Major home systems (HVAC, plumbing, roof, appliances) fail on 15-25 year cycles, but first-time buyers have no frame of reference for when or how much replacement costs
3. **The compounding emotional toll** — Anxiety about the next repair accumulates, disrupts sleep, and creates anticipatory dread that affects daily life
4. **Social media vs. reality** — First-time buyers see curated home renovation content, creating unrealistic expectations for what homeownership delivers

**Who This Is For:** Recent first-time homebuyers (within 5 years) feeling financially blindsided; pre-purchase buyers who want realistic cost education; people watching a friend or family member suffer homeownership anxiety and wanting to help.
