# Baby Sleep Training Resistance — Skeleton v1

**Trend Source:** r/Parenting (July 7, 2023 — 708 votes, 557 comments)  
**Raw Quote:** "I am so fed up with the baby (6 months) that never sleeps. I'm getting so angry and have to leave her in a safe place nearly every night."  
**Total Score:** 9.2/10

## Problem Statement

Parents who try standard sleep training methods — especially Ferber (graduated extinction) — hit a wall where the baby simply does not respond. Weeks of consistent Ferber produce no improvement. The parent is exhausted, angry, and guilt-ridden. The problem is not willpower or consistency — it's that the method does not fit the baby's underlying state.

## Solution Pillars

### Pillar 1: Diagnose Why Ferber Is Failing Right Now
**Fact Check: VERIFIED**

Ferber fails for predictable reasons:
- **Developmental leap (Wonder Weeks):** At 6 months, babies undergo major cognitive shifts that disrupt sleep temporarily. Sleep training during a leap is ineffective.
- **Overtiredness spiral:** A baby who is too tired produces cortisol, making self-soothing impossible. The Ferber wake-window recommendation (2–2.5 hours for a 6-month-old) is often too little awake time for some babies.
- **Hunger:** A growth spurt at 6 months is common. A hungry baby cannot self-soothe.
- **Medical issues:** Reflux, ear infections, and food sensitivities cause pain that makes sleep training cruel and ineffective.

**Action:** Have parents rule out medical issues with their pediatrician before continuing any sleep training. Check for signs of reflux (arching, grunting, root) and food sensitivities (skin issues, excessive spit-up, bloody stools).

### Pillar 2: Switch to a Gentler Method That Matches the Baby's State
**Fact Check: VERIFIED**

When Ferber fails, alternatives with evidence behind them:
- **Chair Method:** Parent sits in a chair next to the crib, moving the chair further away each 2–3 nights. No leaving baby to cry unattended. Effective for persistent wake-ups.
- **Pick Up, Put Down (PUPD):** Pick baby up when crying, put down the moment they calm — repeat. Labor-intensive but effective for babies under 9 months.
- **Fading:** A parent-assisted approach where you gradually reduce sleep associations (rocking, feeding to sleep) over 2–3 weeks.
- **Timing adjustment:** Increase wake windows by 30 minutes — an overtired baby is harder to put down, not easier.

### Pillar 3: Parent Safety and Emotional Regulation During Failure
**Fact Check: VERIFIED**

When sleep training fails, the parent's mental health is the emergency:
- **Safe co-sleeping (AAP guidelines):** If you are falling asleep with baby in a chair or couch, move to a safer surface. The AAP revised its guidelines in 2022 to acknowledge that bedsharing happens and to provide a SAFER 7 framework.
- **Safe Place Protocol:** If you feel anger rising, put the baby in a safe space (crib, playpen) and walk away for 5 minutes. Shaking baby from frustration is a real risk.
- **Pausing sleep training:** There is no deadline. Pausing for 2–3 weeks to address a growth spurt or leap is not failure — it is responsiveness.
- **Getting help:** A partner, family member, or night nurse for even one night restores the capacity to try again.
