## PROBLEM DIAGNOSIS

**Core Problem:** Homeowners and renters dealing with recurring ant infestations who cannot afford professional exterminator services (typically $50-$200/month for ongoing contracts). They've tried spray pesticides and store-bought traps with no lasting results. The frustration is real: they see ants daily, feel invaded in their own home, and don't know whether to trust the borax method, the diatomaceous earth approach, or something else entirely.

**Root Cause Cluster:**
1. **Wrong treatment method** — spray-killing visible ants does nothing to the colony; the queen keeps reproducing
2. **No entry point sealing** — treating inside without closing off how ants enter guarantees recurring reinvasion
3. **Wrong ant type** — odorous house ants, carpenter ants, sugar ants, and pavement ants each respond to different treatments; generic advice fails
4. **Inconsistent protocol** — bait stations work slowly (7-14 days); most people remove them after 2 days thinking they failed

**Who This Is For:** Homeowners and renters who need to eliminate an ant problem for under $30 using supplies from a hardware store or Amazon. They've already tried the obvious and failed. They want a clear, step-by-step system — not a biology lesson.

**Differentiation from existing catalog:** No existing product covers budget DIY ant elimination. Existing products cover plumbing, roof, water damage. This fills a clear home maintenance gap.

## SOLUTION PILLARS

### Pillar 1: Ant ID + Entry Point Sealing (Verified)
**Claim:** Identifying the ant species and locating entry points are the two steps most people skip — and skipping them is why treatments fail.
**Evidence:** University extension services (University of Minnesota, University of California IPM) all state that ant control requires identifying the ant type before selecting a treatment. Odorous house ants (the most common indoor invader) respond to different baits than carpenter ants.
**Status:** VERIFIED — supported by Extension service literature.

### Pillar 2: Slow-Acting Bait Protocol (Verified)
**Claim:** Spray pesticides kill visible ants but leave the colony intact. Slow-acting liquid ant baits (borax-based or indoxacarb) are carried back to the nest, eliminating the colony in 7-14 days.
**Evidence:** EPA IPM guidelines and multiple university extension programs recommend gel baits or borax-sugar baits as primary treatment. Studies show bait acceptance rates of 70-90% when placed correctly.
**Status:** VERIFIED — supported by EPA IPM guidelines and university pest management programs.

### Pillar 3: Exterior Perimeter + Prevention (Plausible)
**Claim:** Treating just the interior guarantees reinvasion. An exterior perimeter barrier prevents new colonies from establishing near the home.
**Evidence:** Most pest control professionals treat exterior foundations as standard practice. The evidence is observational and industry-standard, not peer-reviewed.
**Status:** PLAUSIBLE — standard industry practice, logical but limited peer-reviewed data on efficacy at consumer scale.
