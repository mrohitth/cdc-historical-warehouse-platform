## 3. PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE

### Part 1: Emergency Triage — Is This a Medical Crisis?

**Chapter 1: The 48-Hour Emergency Checklist**
*The reader will be able to:* Identify warning signs that require immediate vet attention: straining to urinate (especially male cats), blood in urine or stool, sudden lethargy, crying in the litter box. Know when to go to the emergency vet tonight vs. schedule a regular appointment.

**Chapter 2: Understanding the Medical Menu**
*The reader will be able to:* Explain the 6 most common medical causes of house soiling: urinary tract infections, feline lower urinary tract disease (FLUTD), kidney disease, diabetes, digestive issues (IBD, constipation), arthritis in senior cats. Know what tests the vet should run and what questions to ask.

**Chapter 3: Vet Visit Preparation Kit**
*The reader will be able to:* Bring a symptom journal, urine sample, and list of all litter boxes/changes to the vet. Know what "urinalysis," "cystocentesis," and "blood panel" results mean for their cat's diagnosis.

---

### Part 2: The Litter Box Audit — Finding What Your Cat Hates

**Chapter 4: The 5-Second Litter Box Test**
*The reader will be able to:* Evaluate their current litter box against 5 standards: size, cleanliness, location, litter type, and number of boxes. Identify the single biggest setup failure within 5 minutes.

**Chapter 5: Box Size and Style Prescription**
*The reader will be able to:* Know exactly what size box their cat needs (1.5x cat length minimum), whether a hooded vs. open box matters for their cat, and when multiple boxes are required (especially in multi-level homes or multi-cat households).

**Chapter 6: Litter Type Decoder**
*The reader will be able to:* Match litter to cat preferences — clumping vs. non-clumping, depth of litter, scent vs. unscented, dust levels. Run a litter preference test at home to discover what their cat actually prefers.

**Chapter 7: Location, Location, Location**
*The reader will be able to:* Identify 3 common location mistakes (near loud machines, in corners cats feel trapped, too far from living areas), evaluate their current box placement, and find better locations in their home.

---

### Part 3: Behavioral Solutions — Environmental Enrichment

**Chapter 8: Stress Mapping Your Home**
*The reader will be able to:* Identify environmental stressors: new pets, moved furniture, construction noise, stray cats outside windows, litter boxes near other pets' resources. Create a "stress inventory" of their household.

**Chapter 9: Feline Environmental Needs (The 5 Pillars)**
*The reader will be able to:* Apply the 5 pillars of cat environmental enrichment: safe spaces, multiple resources, play opportunity, human interaction on cat's terms, control over environment. Implement at least 3 changes within 1 week.

**Chapter 10: Cleaning Enzymatic Proofing**
*The reader will be able to:* Use blacklight (UV flashlight) to find all soiled spots, apply enzymatic cleaner correctly (saturate, wait, blot — don't rub), and prevent re-marking through proper cleaning technique.

---

### Part 4: The Recovery Plan — Building New Habits

**Chapter 11: Confinement Retraining Protocol**
*The reader will be able to:* Safely confine cat to a small area with litter box, food, and safe spaces for 2-4 weeks to rebuild litter habits. Transition back to full house access gradually.

**Chapter 12: Monitoring Progress and Preventing Relapse**
*The reader will be able to:* Track improvements with a daily log, know when to celebrate vs. escalate, and maintain gains through ongoing environmental management.
