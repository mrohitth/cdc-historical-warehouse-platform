## PROBLEM DIAGNOSIS

**Core Problem:** Parents of toddlers (18mo–3.5yr) with strong sleep-onset dependencies (nursing, rocking, co-sleeping) face nightly battles that last 1–2+ hours. The toddler fights sleep, parents feel guilty and exhausted, and no solution holds from night to night.

**Root Cause Cluster:**
1. **Dependency anchor** — toddler has learned to associate sleep onset with a specific parent action (nurse, rock, lie down with them)
2. **Boundary confusion** — toddler tests limits at bedtime because nap boundaries were never established or shifted
3. **Sleep pressure mismatch** — overtired toddlers paradoxically fight sleep harder due to cortisol release
4. **Environmental ambiguity** — toddler doesn't have a consistent "this means sleep" signal

**Who This Is For:** Parents of toddlers who can self-transition at bedtime but refuse to, and who want a gradual, connection-first approach (not extinction/CIO).

## SOLUTION PILLARS

### Pillar 1: The Wind-Down Sequence (Verified)
**Claim:** A consistent, 20-30 minute pre-sleep routine reduces sleep onset battles by creating predictable signal sequence.
**Evidence:** Jodi Mindell et al. research on positive sleep routines. Routines alone have measurable benefits on sleep latency.
**Status:** VERIFIED — supported by peer-reviewed research.

**What to include:**
- Exact order: Bath → PJs → Story → Song → Lie-down cue phrase
- Why each step matters (predictability = security)
- How to handle protests at each transition
- Script for the "goodnight phrase"

### Pillar 2: The Chair Method — Gradual Withdrawal (Verified)
**Claim:** Moving the parent spot progressively further from the bed over 2–3 weeks reduces toddler distress while building independent sleep.
**Evidence:** Recommended by pediatric sleep specialists; used in numerous gentle sleep programs.
**Status:** VERIFIED — widely accepted in pediatric sleep medicine.

**What to include:**
- 5-step chair withdrawal sequence (bed → beside bed → mid-room chair → door → hallway)
- Timing: each stage 3–5 nights before moving
- What to say at each stage (script provided)
- How to handle regression nights

### Pillar 3: The Sleep Pressure Reset (Plausible with nuance)
**Claim:** Overtiredness raises cortisol and causes paradoxically worse sleep. Adjusting nap timing can reduce bedtime battles.
**Evidence:** Sleep science supports the relationship between cortisol/adrenaline and sleep resistance in toddlers. Nap timing matters.
**Status:** PLAUSIBLE — mechanism is sound, individual variation is high.

**What to include:**
- How to assess if nap is too short or too late
- Minimum wake windows by age (18mo, 2yr, 2.5yr, 3yr)
- Signs of overtiredness vs. under-tiredness
- What to do when regression hits
