## 3. PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE

### Part 1: Understanding — Why the Midnight Chaos Happens

**Chapter 1: What Are Zoomies (And Why Your Cat Gets Them)**
*The reader will be able to:* Understand FRAPs (Frenetic Random Activity Periods), know the biological drivers (hunting cycle, cortisol spikes, pent-up daytime energy), and recognize what's normal vs. what's excessive.

**Chapter 2: The Evening Energy Audit**
*The reader will be able to:* Map their cat's current daily energy expenditure, identify gaps in play and stimulation, and determine the right baseline for their cat's age and breed.

**Chapter 3: Breed and Age Factors**
*The reader will be able to:* Know whether their cat's zoomies are influenced by breed (Bengal, Savannah, Abyssinian — higher energy), age (kittens and young cats zoom more), or environment (indoor-only cats with no hunting outlet).

---

### Part 2: The Evening Protocol — Weeks 1-4

**Chapter 4: Pre-Zoomies Play Session (The Core Fix)**
*The reader will be able to:* Execute a 15-20 minute interactive play session 2-3 hours before their own bedtime, using the right toys (wand toys, Da Bird!, laser pointer with treat finale), to deplete the cat's hunting energy before the night cycle begins.

**Chapter 5: The Late-Night Meal Reset**
*The reader will be able to:* Use a timed automatic feeder to shift their cat's largest caloric intake to the evening, aligning natural post-hunt rest with human sleep time. Know the ideal feeding window.

**Chapter 6: Environmental Setup for Nighttime Calm**
*The reader will be able to:* Restructure the cat's nighttime environment: reduce high-perch access to the bedroom, add vertical spaces away from human sleeping areas, use calming pheromone diffusers (Feliway), and eliminate accessible objects that become zoomie targets.

**Chapter 7: Week-by-Week Progression Log**
*The reader will be able to:* Track their cat's zoomie intensity and frequency daily, using the Zoomie Tracker worksheet, to identify what's working and when to escalate.

---

### Part 3: Safety and Damage Control

**Chapter 8: Cat-Proofing for Midnight Sprints**
*The reader will be able to:* Identify the most dangerous zoomie crash zones (shelving, nightstands, fragile objects), secure or remove hazards before the evening protocol takes effect, and prevent injury during the transition period.

**Chapter 9: When Zoomies Signal Something Else**
*The reader will be able to:* Distinguish normal FRAPs from anxiety-driven hyperactivity, compulsive grooming during zoomies (possible OCD), or post-toilet zoomies that might indicate GI discomfort.

---

### Part 4: Maintenance and Realistic Expectations

**Chapter 10: The 4-Week Protocol Summary**
*The reader will be able to:* Follow the condensed 4-week checklist to implement the full protocol independently.

**Chapter 11: Long-Term Maintenance**
*The reader will be able to:* Maintain results without daily intensive effort, adjust for life changes (moving, new pet, schedule shift), and know when to expect regression and how to recover quickly.
