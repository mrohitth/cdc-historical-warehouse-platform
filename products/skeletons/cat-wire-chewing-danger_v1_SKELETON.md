## 3. PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE

### Part 1: Understanding — Why Cats Chew Cords

**Chapter 1: Why Cords Are Irresistible to Cats**
*The reader will be able to:* Understand the sensory appeal of cords (texture, movement, taste, sound), distinguish between playful chewing and compulsive chewing, and know when cord-chewing might signal a medical or behavioral issue.

**Chapter 2: The Real Danger — Electrocution, Burns, and House Fires**
*The reader will be able to:* Understand the actual risk levels: low-voltage vs. high-voltage, what happens physiologically when a cat bites a live wire, documented cases of cat electrocution, and fire risk from cord damage.

**Chapter 3: Kitten vs. Adult Cat — Different Causes, Different Fixes**
*The reader will be able to:* Know whether their cat's chewing is likely developmental (kittens teething, exploring), attention-seeking, or compulsive. Different causes require different interventions.

---

### Part 2: The Cord Safety System — Physical Management

**Chapter 4: Cord Covering Solutions That Actually Work**
*The reader will be able to:* Install cord covers, cable sleeves, and cord channels that cats cannot access. Includes specific product recommendations and DIY alternatives for every room.

**Chapter 5: Room-by-Room Cord Audit**
*The reader will be able to:* Walk through their home and identify every cord, cable, and wire — including behind furniture and in corners. Categorize by risk level and prioritize protection.

**Chapter 6: Outdoor and Garage Cord Hazards**
*The reader will be able to:* Identify extension cords, holiday lights, and outdoor equipment cords that pose outdoor risk, and apply temporary protection strategies.

---

### Part 3: Behavioral Redirection — Stopping the Urge

**Chapter 7: Alternative Chewing Outlets**
*The reader will be able to:* Provide appropriate cat-safe chewing alternatives: cat grass, commercial chew toys, textured surfaces. Position them near previously targeted cords as substitutes.

**Chapter 8: Taste Aversion That Actually Works**
*The reader will be able to:* Apply effective bitter-tasting deterrents correctly — why most sprays fail (wrong application, cats habituate, wrong active ingredients), which products work (Grannick's Bitter Apple, Försegla, cayenne pepper solution), and how to apply for maximum effectiveness.

**Chapter 9: Play Therapy for Cord-Chasing Cats**
*The reader will be able to:* Reduce cord-chewing motivation through structured play that satisfies hunting instinct, reducing the "everything moves and must be attacked" drive.

**Chapter 10: Attention and Departure Protocol**
*The reader will be able to:* Reduce separation-anxiety-driven cord chewing through departure routines and environmental enrichment when cat is home alone.

---

### Part 4: Emergency Preparedness and Long-Term Management

**Chapter 11: If Your Cat Is Electrocuted — What to Do**
*The reader will be able to:* Recognize signs of electrical shock (burns in mouth, difficulty breathing, cardiac arrhythmia), administer first aid, and get to an emergency vet immediately.

**Chapter 12: The 30-Day Safety Plan**
*The reader will be able to:* Implement the full system in 30 days with a structured checklist covering all rooms, products, and behavioral steps.

**Chapter 13: Long-Term Maintenance**
*The reader will be able to:* Maintain cord safety as cat ages, when moving to new home, during holidays with new cords (Christmas lights), and after household changes.
