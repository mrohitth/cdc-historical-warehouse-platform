## Description (keyword-rich)
Adopting a cat and feeling like you made a mistake is more common than you think. If your new cat hides, flinches, or seems distant—you're not failing. This guide covers why the weeks after adoption are actually the hardest part, how cats show affection in ways dog owners miss, and why your cat is likely already bonding with you. Includes signs of cat love (slow blink, following at a distance, head-butts) and what to expect in weeks 2-8. You're not broken. Your cat isn't broken. You both just need time.
