**Title:** My cat bites me unprovoked. Here's what I learned after spending hours researching and talking to a behaviorist.

**Body:**

I want to share what I learned because I know there are other people out there dealing with this and feeling trapped. I was one of you six months ago.

My cat (6-year-old female, spayed) started biting me about a year ago. Not play biting—real bites that broke skin. I never knew when it would happen. She'd be on my lap purring and then suddenly she'd latch onto my hand. I have scars on my wrist from one episode.

I tried everything I read online: ignoring her when she bit, hissing at her (stupid), putting her in another room, redirecting with toys. Nothing worked consistently.

What finally helped was understanding that biting is a symptom, not a diagnosis. There are different types of cat aggression, and each requires a different approach.

**What I learned:**

1. **Petting-induced aggression** is real. Cats have a threshold for touch. Mine can tolerate about 2 minutes of belly rubs before she switches from enjoying it to defending herself. I was missing the warning signs (tail flick, ear rotation, skin rippling) because I wasn't looking for them.

2. **Play aggression** is common in single-cat households. If your cat attacks hands and feet, she's treating them like prey. The solution is structured prey-sequence play—hunt, catch, kill, eat—using a wand toy for 15 minutes before bed.

3. **A vet visit ruled out medical causes.** Dental pain, arthritis, thyroid issues—all can cause defensive aggression. This step should come before any behavioral intervention.

**The free resource I made:**

I put together a simple checklist that walks you through identifying your cat's aggression type, the vet visit checklist, and the prey sequence protocol. No tricks, no email list—just a helpful document I wish I'd had.

Comment "checklist" and I'll send it to you directly.

I know how isolating this can feel. I'm not saying every cat can be fixed—but I'm saying there are things you can try that you might not have tried yet.
