## Description
Your HOA wasn't always like this. Three board members resigned, new people ran unopposed, and suddenly you have a $20 fee for guests and your cat can't go outside. You're not crazy — this is a documented pattern that happens when HOA culture shifts. This guide gives you the playbook: how to read your HOA documents and find leverage, what the board can actually legally enforce versus what they're bluffing about, how to run for the board and win even without prior experience, and if leaving is your only option — how to sell without hemorrhaging your home's value.
