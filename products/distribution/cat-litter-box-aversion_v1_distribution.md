**Title:** What I learned solving my cat's house soiling problem (after months of frustration)

**Post Body:**

My cat started peeing outside the litter box about 6 months ago. Full stop. Not the box, not nearby — on the carpet, on my bed, on the couch. I'd cleaned everything, tried different litters, even yelled at her (which made me feel terrible and didn't help).

What I learned: cats don't eliminate outside the box out of spite. They're trying to tell you something is wrong.

Here's what finally worked for us:

**Rule out medical first.** Before you change anything about litter or environment, get your cat checked by a vet. In my case, it was a UTI. Easy to treat, and the house soiling stopped within a week of antibiotics.

**Audit the litter box setup.** The vet cleared her medically, which meant the problem was environmental. Turns out my box was too small (seriously — I didn't know there were size guidelines), and I'd been keeping it too far from her favorite areas.

**The enzymatic cleaner thing is real.** I tried regular cleaners for months. The enzymatic ones actually work because they break down the urine proteins at a chemical level. Blacklight flashlight helped me find spots I didn't even know were there.

**Stress was part of it.** We had gotten a new couch that summer. Turns out cats can be stressed by furniture rearrangements in ways we don't think about.

I'm not saying this is a magic fix — some cats have more complex issues. But the medical screening and litter box audit together identified the cause in our case.
