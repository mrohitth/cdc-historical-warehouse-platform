**Title:** What actually worked for ending screen time tantrums (no judgment zone)

**Body:**

I'll start by saying: I gave my kids more screen time than I wanted to during a hard season. We had a new baby, I was working from home with no childcare, and the iPad became the default. No judgment please—I'm here about solutions.

When I finally decided to set limits, the tantrums were worse than I expected. Like, full-hour meltdown worse. I tried cold turkey and lasted two days before caving. I tried "just 30 more minutes" and it extended to two hours. Everything I tried made it worse.

What finally worked was a system instead of willpower. I want to share it in case it helps someone else in the same spot.

**The 5-2-1 warning system:**
- 5 minutes: "Hey, you have 5 more minutes"
- 2 minutes: "Two minutes, get ready to pause"
- 1 minute: "One minute, find a stopping point"

The key is: when the timer goes off, the device goes away. Not because I said so—because time ran out. I'm not arguing anymore.

I also started using a token system. My 6-year-old gets 3 stars per day. One star = 30 minutes. When they're gone, they're gone until tomorrow. No borrowing against tomorrow. No "please just this once."

The first week was brutal—extinction burst is real. But by week two, something shifted. She started pacing herself. She asks "should I use a star now or later?" She checks her chart before asking for the iPad.
