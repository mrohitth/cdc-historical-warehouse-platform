**Post Body:**

Had a conversation with a plumber last month about drain clogs (specifically bathroom drains — shower, sink, tub). He said something that stuck: "The problem isn't the clog. The problem is the stuff on the pipe walls that keeps making new clogs."

Most people clear a clog, feel good about it, and then get frustrated when it comes back 6 weeks later. The reason it comes back: chemical drain cleaners dissolve the blockage but leave the residue lining the pipe. Soap scum from bar soap (sodium tallowate + water minerals) builds up layer by layer. Hair catches on that residue and forms a new clog.

Here's what actually works:

**1. Switch from bar soap to liquid body wash.**
Bar soap is the main cause of soap scum buildup in bathroom pipes. This one change reduces future buildup significantly.

**2. Install hair catchers and actually clean them.**
Not just when the drain is slow — every 2-3 uses. A weekly 2-minute clean prevents the hair accumulation that causes most clogs.

**3. Use enzymatic drain cleaners monthly instead of chemical cleaners when things are working fine.**
Enzymatic cleaners (BioClean, Green Gobler) digest the organic residue on pipe walls. Chemical cleaners dissolve clogs but don't prevent future ones, and overuse can damage pipes.

**4. When you do have a clog, plunge correctly.**
Block the overflow hole (the second opening on most bathroom sinks/tubs). Without blocking it, the pressure escapes and the plunge does nothing.

**The 15-minute monthly routine that keeps drains clear:**
- Enzymatic treatment once a month (pour in, let sit overnight, flush with hot water)
- Clean hair catchers weekly (2 minutes)
- Deep clean drain stoppers monthly (remove, wash, reassemble)
