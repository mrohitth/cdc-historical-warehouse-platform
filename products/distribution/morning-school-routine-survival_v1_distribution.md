**Title:** The system that saved our mornings (moving 80% of prep to the night before)

**Body:**

I'll be honest: I used to dread mornings. Getting three kids out the door by 7:45 was a daily war zone. Screaming, crying, lost shoes, forgotten lunches, me showing up to work already depleted. I felt like a failure every single morning.

Then I stopped trying to manage the morning and started moving the work to the night before.

**The launchpad system changed everything:**

Everything my kids need for school goes into a bin by the door: shoes, jacket, backpack. The rule is: when you get home from school, the first thing you do is put your stuff in your bin. Everything in, nothing stays in the bedroom.

Before bed (8pm), we run the checklist:
- Backpack packed
- Lunch in the fridge (either made or components ready)
- Clothes laid out for tomorrow
- Shoes by the door
- One last check

This takes about 20 minutes now that it's a habit. The first two weeks took 45 minutes each night.

**Why this works:**

Mornings are chaos because they're rushed. The brain isn't rested enough for decision-making. Moving prep to the evening means mornings are just execution, not planning.

My 7-year-old now runs her own morning routine with a visual chart. She wakes up, checks the chart, does the steps. I don't nag. The chart nags for me (well, it's more like a visual reminder that she actually looks at, which is more than she ever did with my verbal reminders).

**The free checklist:**

I made a printable that walks you through the launchpad setup, the night-before checklist, and the morning chart. It's the document I created for my own family and it's been shared with about 200 other parents who asked for it.

This isn't about being a perfect parent. It's about having a system so you're not starting every day already defeated.
