I'm posting this because I genuinely want to prevent someone else from going through the 20 minutes of pure terror I experienced last month.

My cat bit through my laptop charging cable while it was plugged in. Not frayed — it was fine that morning. I was in the next room. I heard him yowl and came running. He was fine (thank god), but for those few seconds, he was chewing on a live 110V cable.

I didn't know this: a cat's moist mouth and tongue conduct household current efficiently. Electrocution can occur within seconds. Cats have died from this. House fires from pet cord chewing are tracked by the NFPA.

What I tried before that didn't work:
- Bitter apple spray (he ate through it)
- Dish soap on the cord (he didn't care)
- Yelling "no" (he associated it with attention)

Here's what actually works — a complete system, not piecemeal advice:

Physical protection first: Cable sleeves (braided PET tubing, $10-15 for a roll) go around every accessible cord in my apartment. Cord channels for the permanent installations (behind the TV, along the desk). Cable boxes for power strips. The cord is physically inaccessible. No amount of determined chewing gets through.

Taste aversion second: Grannick's Bitter Apple for Cats specifically — it's formulated for feline taste receptors, not just bitter to humans. Generic bitter sprays didn't work. The cat-specific formulation did. Apply every 2-3 days and after cleaning.

Alternative chewing outlets: I keep cat grass near his favorite cord-target areas. He goes for the grass now. Plus two structured play sessions per day — I learned that cord-chasing is often misfired hunting behavior. Playing him out properly reduced the drive to attack moving things at random.

The math: new laptop cable = $80. Cable sleeves for my whole apartment = $40. Automatic feeder so I'm not his food trigger = $35. Cat grass = free to grow. The investment is less than one replaced device.

Free cord safety checklist I made for myself — organized by room so I could audit and fix systematically. It's in my profile for anyone who wants it.

Please, if your cat bites cords, take this seriously. It's not a joke. It's not cute. My cat was lucky. Not every cat is.
