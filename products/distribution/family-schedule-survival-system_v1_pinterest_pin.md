Stop being the only one tracking the family schedule. This practical guide gives you a simple, no-app-required system that actually distributes schedule ownership across your household. You'll learn a shared visibility system so everyone checks for themselves, a weekly review habit that eliminates mid-week surprises, and a triage framework for deciding what goes on the calendar. Perfect for busy families drowning in activities, logistics, and last-minute chaos. Stop answering "what's happening today?" on behalf of everyone.
