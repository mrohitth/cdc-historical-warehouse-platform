My 4-year-old neutered male cat started spraying about 6 months ago. Walls, doors, my bedroom door at 3am. I tried everything the pet store recommended. Pheromone collar. Two different enzymatic cleaners. Feliway diffuser. I spent probably $400+ on products that did nothing.

Took him to the vet. Standard exam, no issues found. But I pushed for a urinalysis specifically because I wasn't satisfied. They found crystals. Calcium oxalate, which is apparently the kind that's most common in neutered male cats. The vet said the spraying was likely irritation from the crystals passing.

Six weeks on a urinary diet and a follow-up culture later and he's stopped completely. Not spraying, not straining. Normal.

I wish I'd gotten the urinalysis done first instead of trying product after product. And I wish I'd known that male cats — especially neutered males — are at higher risk for urinary blockages and FLUTD than female cats. It's not just a behavioral thing.

If your neutered male cat is spraying and you've tried everything, please get the urinalysis. Not just the exam. Specifically the urinalysis and a urine culture. Push for it if your vet seems dismissive.

Not looking for advice — just wanted to share what actually worked in case it helps someone else. I've posted a free checklist of the questions I asked the vet and the warning signs I wish I'd known about in my profile. Feel free to grab it if it's useful.
