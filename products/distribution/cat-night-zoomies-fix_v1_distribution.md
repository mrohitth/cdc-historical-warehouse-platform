My cat used to tear through our apartment at 2, 3, and 4am every night. I was getting about 3-4 hours of sleep. I tried everything.

Short version: I fixed it. Here's what actually worked, no gimmicks.

The core issue: cats are crepuscular (most active at dawn and dusk). If your cat sleeps all day and gets minimal stimulation, that pent-up hunting energy has to go somewhere. The 3am sprint isn't random — it's a pressure release.

What I tried that didn't work:
- Yelling (reinforced the behavior)
- Water spray (temporary, didn't address root cause)
- Closing the bedroom door (howling started)
- Ignoring it (escalated for about a week before plateauing)

What actually worked:

1. Pre-bed play session, 2-3 hours before I went to sleep. Not just tossing a toy — 15-20 minutes with a wand toy in short bursts. Three 5-minute sessions with 2-minute rests. Let the cat catch the toy at the end of each burst. The catch is the reward. This mimics the hunting sequence: hunt, catch, eat, rest.

2. Shifted the biggest meal to 30-60 minutes before my bedtime. Used an automatic feeder so I wasn't the food trigger at 6am. Post-meal grogginess lined up with my own sleep window.

3. Pheromone diffuser in the bedroom (Feliway Classic). Not a magic solution but reduced ambient anxiety that was amplifying the hyperactive episodes.

4. Cat-proofed the sprint path during the 2-week transition. Removed breakables from nightstands. Closed the bathroom door. Interrupted the sprint cycle before it built full momentum.
