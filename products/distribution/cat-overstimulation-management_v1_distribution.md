**[ADVICE] My cat became a menace out of nowhere — here's what actually helped**

I posted here a few months ago about being at my absolute limit with my 5-year-old cat. He was knocking everything off counters, yowling at 3am, biting without warning, and I was crying every other day because I was so frustrated.

I want to share what actually helped — because the comments I got ranged from "just play with him more" (we were) to "rehome him" (not what I needed to hear).

**The thing nobody told me: medical first.**

I took him to the vet thinking it was behavioral. They found nothing. I pushed for a senior blood panel and urinalysis anyway because something felt wrong. Turned out he had early-stage hyperthyroidism. After starting treatment, his nighttime yowling dropped by 80% within 3 weeks. All the "behavioral" problems were pain and discomfort I couldn't see.

If your adult cat suddenly changed behavior, please do the blood panel before you try any behavioral intervention.

**After medical was cleared, the Hunt-Catch-Kill-Eat cycle changed everything.**

I wasn't playing with him wrong. I wasn't playing long enough. Two 15-minute structured wand toy sessions per day — letting him actually catch the toy at the end — plus kibble in a puzzle feeder instead of a bowl. He's a different cat 6 weeks later.

**The guilt I felt:** I spent months feeling like a failure for resenting my own pet. That feeling is more common than the posts about it let on. You're not alone. It's not character. It's usually solvable.
