## Reddit Bridge Post

**Subreddit target:** r/CatAdvice

**Post title:** Litter Robot died after warranty expired — what actually worked for me

**Post body:**

My Litter Robot 3 died 3 months past warranty. Error code, grinding motor, cats refused to touch the backup tray for two weeks. I went through the full repair vs. replace decision and learned a lot that I wish I'd known on day one.

Here's what actually helped:

**Diagnose before you replace.** Most error codes have documented DIY fixes. E1/E2/E3 on Litter Robot models are usually sensor issues — clean the sensors with compressed air, not just a wipe. Belt broke on mine. $11 belt from Amazon, 15 minutes of YouTube, fixed.

**The 50% rule for replace vs. repair.** If the repair costs more than 50% of a new unit AND your device is over 2 years old, just replace. For Litter Robot 4, that's roughly $350. For Litter Robot 3, $275.

**Bridge care matters.** If your cat has been without a working box, set up the simplest possible tray — plain, uncovered, clumping litter, 2-3 inches deep, scooped daily. Don't add anything fancy. Cats don't need aromatherapy litter or automatic anything. They need a clean, accessible bathroom.

**Watch for urinary blockage in male cats.** If your male cat is straining to urinate and nothing is coming out — emergency vet today. Not tomorrow.

I put together a more complete guide to the whole process — diagnosing the failure, the DIY fix costs, repair vs. replace decision framework, and the cat behavioral piece. It's free and has a printable checklist.
