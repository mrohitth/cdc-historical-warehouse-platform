# DISTRIBUTION: Parent Meal Prep Monotony

## Reddit Bridge Post

**Subreddit:** r/Parenting (or r/toddlers, r/food)

**Title:** Escape the short order cook trap: A system that cuts weekday meal prep to 10 minutes

**Body:**

I spent years making three separate meals every night. Separate pasta for my kid, different sauce for my spouse, and whatever I could throw together for myself. The idea of doing this for another decade made me want to cry.

I built a system that changed this. Here's what worked:

**The 2-hour Sunday session:**
- Batch cook foods that freeze and reheat well (meatballs, pancakes, roasted veggies)
- Label with dates, freeze in portions
- Weekday prep becomes 10-15 minutes of assembly

**The 4-week rotation:**
- 15 dinners that kids actually eat
- Posted on the fridge so there's no "what's for dinner" debate
- Kids help choose from the list, which means they have ownership

**The survival kit for impossible days:**
- Always include one safe food (no guilt)
- Store-bought pizza is valid
- Calling backup is allowed

The core insight: you don't need better recipes. You need a system that removes the daily decision load.

I made a free checklist for the Sunday session if anyone wants it. No email, no sales pitch — just a Google doc.
