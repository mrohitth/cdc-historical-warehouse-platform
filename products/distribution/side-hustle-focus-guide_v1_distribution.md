# DISTRIBUTION: Side Hustle Focus Guide

## Reddit Bridge Post (r/SideProject)

**Title:** Six months, multiple projects, exhausted. Here's the framework that helped me pick one.

**Body:**

I've been there.

Six months ago I had 5 active projects. A dropshipping store. A freelance copywriting gig. A YouTube channel. A print-on-demand shop. A consulting offer I never launched.

I was working every evening and weekend. My bank account didn't reflect the effort.

The problem wasn't working hard. It was working on too many things at once.

What changed: the one-project framework.

**The audit:**
I wrote down every project, hours invested, and income generated. 300+ hours across 5 projects. $47 in total income.

**The four criteria for picking one:**
1. Has it made at least $1? (Revenue proof)
2. Can it launch in 30 days? (Time to market)
3. Does it use skills I'm building? (Skill leverage)
4. Do I actually want to work on it? (Emotional pull)

Pick the project that passes 3 of 4.

**The archive decision:**
I archived everything except one project. Not "paused." Archived. There's a psychological difference.

**The focus system:**
- Morning hour before day job (no phone, no social)
- One metric to improve per week
- Friday kill list: what did I NOT do this week?

Three months later: one finished project generating consistent revenue.

The starting loop is seductive because starting feels productive. It isn't. Finishing is.
