## Reddit Bridge Post (r/dogs)

**Title:** What finally worked for my dog's separation anxiety — a breakdown of the actual protocol

---

I want to share what actually worked for my dog's separation anxiety, because I spent months trying advice that made it worse.

My dog could not be left alone for more than 10 minutes without destroying his crate, howling, and eventually hurting himself trying to get out. I could not go to the grocery store without coming home to a disaster or a neighbor complaint.

Here is what I learned, in case it helps anyone else in the same position:

**1. Separation anxiety is a panic disorder, not bad behavior.**

Your dog is not acting out because he is mad at you. His body goes into fight-or-flight when you leave. This changes how you respond to it — punishment does not work, "just leaving him more" makes it worse, and crate training alone is not a fix.

**2. The actual protocol is systematic desensitization.**

You need to leave your dog for a duration so short that anxiety does not peak. Then return. Repeat dozens of times, gradually extending the duration. I started at 30 seconds. It took 8 weeks, but he now handles 4 hours.

**3. The Julie Naismith "Leave Games" are genuinely useful.**

These are pre-departure exercises that teach your dog to associate your departure cues with positive things, before you ever actually leave. Worth looking into.

**4. Get a camera if you do not have one.**

You need to see exactly when your dog crosses from anticipation into panic. I used a Wyze cam. It was the diagnostic tool I needed.

**5. Know when to get professional help.**

If your dog is hurting himself, or if you have been at this for 8+ weeks with no improvement, see a veterinary behaviorist. Some dogs need pharmaceutical support alongside the behavioral protocol. This is not failure — it is medicine.

I put together a full guide covering everything from understanding the panic response to the graduated departure protocol to knowing when to escalate. It is free to download — there is a checklist that goes with it that makes it easy to track your progress.

Happy to answer questions if anyone is in the middle of this. It gets better.
