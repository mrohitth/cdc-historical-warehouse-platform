## Description (keyword-rich)
You've launched Project #4. Before that, #3. Before that, #2. Sound familiar?

This guide is for side hustlers stuck in the starting loop who can't point to a single finished project.

Inside, you'll learn why starting triggers the same dopamine hit as finishing (without the income), how to run a project audit that shows your real numbers, and a simple categorization system to separate what deserves your time from what's holding you back.

Most people find they've spent 200+ hours across multiple projects and made less than $100. This isn't a guilt trip. It's a reality check that shows the actual cost of staying in the starting loop—and how to break out of it.

If you've attended more webinars about side hustles than you've shipped products, this is your wake-up call.
