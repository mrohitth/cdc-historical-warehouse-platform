**Title:** The vet appointment system that actually changed things for my terrified cat

My cat was so aggressive at the vet that appointments kept getting cancelled. Not dramatic — literally cancelled, mid-way, because they couldn't safely handle her.

After years of this cycle, I finally built a system that works. Not "relaxation tips" or "play with your cat more." Actual, structural changes.

Here's what actually moved the needle:

**1. The carrier is the problem, not the cat**

My carrier only came out for vet visits. My cat learned: carrier = bad. Now it lives in the living room with the door open, a blanket inside, and treats near it. We feed meals inside occasionally. The association is gone.

**2. Gabapentin — ask your vet**

My vet prescribed gabapentin (yes, the human nerve drug) for pre-visit sedation. There's a 2017 JAVMA study backing this up. One dose 60–90 minutes before the appointment. My cat is still conscious but significantly calmer. It's not knockout sedation — it's dial-down-the-panic sedation.

**3. Mobile vets for severe cases**

For dental work and blood draws, we now use a mobile vet. At home, in familiar territory, my cat is a different animal. It's more expensive (roughly 1.5–2x clinic rates) but it gets done without anyone getting hurt.

**What didn't work:** Feliway alone, "gentle handling" at the clinic, rescue remedies.

**What I built from this** is a free checklist that walks through the carrier training schedule, the gabapentin pre-visit checklist, and what to ask your vet. It's not a sales thing — it's the system I use. Drop a comment if you want the link.
