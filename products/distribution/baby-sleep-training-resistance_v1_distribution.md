**[VENT/ADVICE] Ferber method failed us completely at 6 months — what finally worked**

My baby is 7 months now. We did Ferber for 3 weeks, completely by the book. Intervals, no picking up, the whole protocol. Zero improvement. She was still waking every 60–90 minutes by night 21.

I was losing it. Not metaphorically — I was getting to a place where I had to put her down and walk away because I could feel the anger coming. That's when I knew something had to change.

Here's what I learned the hard way:

**1. Check for medical first.** Reflux and cow's milk protein allergy both cause sleep disruption that no sleep training will fix. Our pediatrician initially dismissed it. We pushed for a trial of hypoallergenic formula and within a week, her sleep improved. Not because of the training — because she was in pain before.

**2. Wake windows are a range, not a rule.** The 2–2.5 hour wake window for a 6-month-old is an average. Some babies genuinely need 3+ hours of awake time to be tired enough to fall asleep. We extended by 30 minutes and the fighting stopped almost immediately.

**3. There's no deadline.** We paused training for 2 weeks to address the food sensitivity. We resumed. The pause didn't erase anything. We ended up using a modified Chair Method and it worked better than Ferber ever did.

If you're in the same boat — Ferber isn't working and you feel like you're failing — you're not. The method might be wrong for your baby right now. That's all.
