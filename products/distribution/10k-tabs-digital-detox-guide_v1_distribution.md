# DISTRIBUTION: 10K Tabs Digital Detox Guide

## Reddit Bridge Post (r/productivity)

**Title:** I had 3,000 browser tabs open. Here's the system that cleared them in 3 weeks.

**Body:**

Three weeks ago, I exported my browser tabs and counted them.

3,247.

Not a flex. A cry for help.

My browser took 10 seconds to open. Ctrl+F was useless. I had tabs open on three devices that I hadn't touched in months. Every time I opened my laptop, I felt this low-grade anxiety about the tab bar.

I tried the "just close them" approach. Made it 3 days before I had 200 more open.

What actually worked: a batch processing system that treats tab cleanup like a focused work session, not an endless chore.

**The basics:**
- Export all tabs to bookmarks FIRST (this removes the fear of losing anything)
- Count your tabs so you know what you're dealing with
- Set a 25-minute timer
- Open 10 tabs at a time from your archive
- For each one: Read it now (if <2 min), Bookmark to a specific folder, or Close it
- Stop when the timer stops

**The rules that changed everything:**
- Daily cap: never end the day with more than 20 open tabs
- One-in-one-out: close one tab for every new tab you open
- Weekly review: 10 minutes Friday to close old tabs and consolidate

I went from 3,247 to 340 in three weeks. The 340 are organized and actually useful.

The key insight: tab hoarding is incremental and invisible. The solution has to be systematic, not willpower-based.

If you're in a similar situation, the system works. Happy to share more details.
