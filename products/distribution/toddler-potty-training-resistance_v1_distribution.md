**Title:** The 3-Day Method failed for us too — here's what actually works for resistant toddlers

---

Poster shares their experience with the 3-day method failing: child sits on potty for 20 minutes, gets up, pees on floor 30 seconds later.

My son did the exact same thing at 24 months. We tried everything — naked method, pull-ups, stickers, threats, rewards. Nothing worked.

What I eventually figured out: **most potty training failures aren't behavioral problems — they're readiness problems.** My kid wasn't physically ready to hold it. His bladder was too small.

We stopped training entirely for 3 months. Built readiness instead (language, casual exposure, letting him see us use the toilet). At 27 months, we tried again. It took 6 days.

**The 3 things that actually matter for resistant toddlers:**

1. **Readiness first.** If your child isn't showing 4-5 readiness signs, stop. No method works on a child whose body isn't ready.

2. **Match the method to the kid's temperament.** High-energy kids fail at the naked method because they can't tolerate sitting. Fearful kids fail at reward-based methods because the pressure makes it worse. Strong-willed kids fail at timed sitting because it's a power battle.

3. **Know your resistance pattern.** "Sits but won't go" is a different problem than "fights sitting" or "trained at daycare but not at home." Each has a specific fix.
