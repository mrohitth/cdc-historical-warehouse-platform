Male Cat Spraying After Neutering? Emergency Signs

## Description
Your neutered male cat won't stop spraying? Before you blame behavior, you need to rule out a medical emergency. Neutered males have a narrow urethra that can become blocked, causing FLUTD or urinary obstruction—a life-threatening condition. This guide walks you through emergency warning signs, what tests to request at the vet, and when spraying is actually a symptom, not a behavior problem. Don't waste money on diffusers until you've ruled out crystals, sludge, or blockage. Free checklist in bio.
