My cat did something weird. She'd use the litter box every single time she needed to pee. But poop? Floor only. Right next to the box. Or the bathroom rug. Never random — always deliberate.

I spent months thinking she was doing it on purpose. That she was mad at me. I tried switching litter. Different boxes. Cleaning more. Nothing worked.

Then I learned something that changed how I thought about it: **cats don't eliminate outside the box out of spite.** They do it because something is wrong — and the "pee vs poop" pattern is one of the most specific signals they can give.

Here's what's usually going on when a cat pees in the box but refuses to poop in it:

**1. Constipation (most common)**
Defecation hurts. When it's painful to poop, a cat learns the box = pain. She holds it as long as she can, then finds somewhere else. Hard, dry stools are the tell. Check the litter box — what do the stools actually look like?

**2. Box design issue**
Automatic litter boxes have a problem: the rake can activate while the cat is mid-poop. If that happened even once, your cat may have decided the box isn't safe for #2. Same with hooded boxes — some cats feel trapped during the longer, more vulnerable act of defecation.

**3. Past negative experience**
A painful constipation episode, getting startled while in the box, even loud construction near the litter box area — cats remember. The box becomes associated with something scary or painful, even if that original problem is now resolved.

**The diagnostic shortcut:** Watch WHERE your cat poops. Right next to the box = "I want to use it but something's wrong with it." Random spots around the house = stress, medical issue, or multi-cat dynamics.

The fix protocol that worked for me:
- Vet visit first (rule out constipation, IBD)
- Added an open box with different litter (not the automatic one)
- Moved to a quieter location
- Started tracking stools with a simple journal

The free checklist I made covers all of this: the emergency vet signs, the poop journal template, and a box audit that takes about 5 minutes.

If you're dealing with this — you're not alone. And no, your cat isn't doing it to spite you.
