**Post Body:**

I had my baby six weeks ago. I haven't slept more than 2 hours at a time since. I know that's normal with a newborn.

---

**Comment (bridges to checklist):**

This sounds like hypervigilance—it's a real thing that affects a lot of new parents. Your nervous system is stuck in threat-assessment mode because birth triggers that "new baby, high danger" mammalian response.

It doesn't mean you're broken. And it does get better.

I put together a free checklist of grounding techniques that can help interrupt the loop—things you can do in 2 minutes while holding the baby. Happy to share if it's helpful.
