### Strategic Issue to Raise with Mathew

With ~20 months of real DE experience, the framing around "mid-level" and "2.5+ years" will raise eyebrows with anyone who asks basic interview questions. The content itself is strong — the CDC pipeline work is genuinely impressive. The issue is framing: lean into **depth and learning trajectory** rather than scale. For example:
- Instead of "2.5+ years" → "~2 years building production DE systems"
- Instead of "mid-level" → "seeking roles where I can grow into senior ownership"
- Instead of "18 months at Freddie Mac" → be precise: "since April 2024 at Capco (embedded with Freddie Mac)"

The site currently sells experience it doesn't quite have. It would be more credible — and more defensible in interviews — to sell the *quality* of the experience instead.
